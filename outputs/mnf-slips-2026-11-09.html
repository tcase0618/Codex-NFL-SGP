What Works

  • The QB is the hub. Receivers only hit together when their QB has a big night (stack hits 1.38× its independent odds).
  • Predictions are calibrated: 13.3% predicted vs 13.9% actual over 3,410 slips.
  • Positive in both halves: +27% (2013–19), +33% (2020–26) under independent pricing.

What Doesn't

  • ATTD parlays: no correlation edge once TD odds are calibrated (0.99×).
  • RB + own QB stacks (0.98×) and receiver-only stacks (1.00×).
  • Game sides/totals: model's edge vs closing lines vanished in 2025–26.

The Rules

  1. One QB-hub stack per game: QB passing yards + two of his own pass-catchers, each line just above projection.
  2. Price decides everything. Bet only when the book pays at least our fair odds.
  3. ≤ 1% of bankroll per slip — sized to survive a 45-slip losing run.
  4. ATTD and lottery slips are fun money, not edge plays.
  5. Log the offered price on every slip. Whether books price the QB–receiver correlation is the single question that decides this strategy.
Bottom line: Slip 1 is a good bet at +830 or better (confidence 7/10 in the method; ~1 in 9 to hit). Below +830, pass.
